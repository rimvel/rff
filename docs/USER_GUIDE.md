# RFF user guide

RFF finds cheap Ryanair flights, including **one-stop trips that Ryanair won't sell you as one booking**.
For example, Vilnius → London → Lisbon on two separate tickets can cost less than any direct flight, or reach a
place with no direct flight at all.

Open it at **https://rff.fvcloud.eu**. It works on phones and computers, needs no account and stores nothing
about you on the server.

> RFF is unofficial and not connected to Ryanair. Prices come from Ryanair's public website, and you always book
> on ryanair.com.

## 1. Quick start

1. In **From**, type an airport, city or country (for example `Vilnius`) and pick it from the list.
2. In **To**, do the same, or pick **Anywhere** to see the cheapest places to go.
3. Choose a **Departure** date. For a return trip, switch to **Round trip** and choose a return date.
4. Press **Search**.

Results appear within a second or two: direct flights first, then connections as they are found.

On the home page, the **Try one** buttons start a ready-made search in one tap:

| Button | What it searches |
|---|---|
| **Anywhere this weekend** | Cheapest places to fly to next Friday, one way |
| **A week in the sun** | A Saturday-to-Saturday trip about two weeks ahead to the warm coasts and islands ("South of Europe"), ±1 day |
| **The next long weekend** | The next Lithuanian long weekend or school break that needs little or no leave, to the same sunny places |

They fly from the airport of your latest search, or from Vilnius the first time.

## 2. Choosing where to fly

The **From** and **To** fields accept more than one airport. Type and pick from the list:

| You type | You get |
|---|---|
| An airport or code: `Stansted`, `STN` | That airport |
| A city: `London`, `Milan` | All of that city's airports (for London: Stansted, Luton, Gatwick and others) |
| A country: `Spain`, `Italy` | Every Ryanair airport in that country. Press Enter on an exact country name to pick it |
| A region or group (below) | A ready-made set of airports |

Ready-made groups:

| Group | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Spain South**, **Spain North**, **Spain Islands** | Andalusia and the Costas; northern Spain; the Balearic and Canary Islands |
| **Canary Islands**, **Balearic Islands** | Just those islands |
| **Italy North**, **Italy South**, **Italian Islands** | Northern Italy; the south; Sicily and Sardinia |
| **Greece Islands**, **Crete** | The Greek islands; Heraklion and Chania |
| **Portugal Coast** | The Algarve and Lisbon |
| **Croatia Coast** | Dalmatia and Kvarner Bay |
| **South of France** | The Riviera, Languedoc and Corsica |
| **South of Europe** | Warm for most of the year: the Canaries, Málaga, Alicante, Murcia, Algarve, Madeira, Sicily, Malta, Crete, Rhodes, Kos, Santorini and Cyprus |
| **Ski & Alps** | Airports near ski resorts: Salzburg, Klagenfurt, Memmingen, Friedrichshafen, Grenoble, Turin, Verona and Treviso |

You can also type words like `sunny`, `beach`, `ski` or `snow` to find these groups.

The ⇄ button swaps From and To.

With a keyboard: the **Up** and **Down** arrows move through the list, **Enter** picks the highlighted item
(or, with nothing highlighted, the best match for what you typed) and **Esc** closes the list.

## 3. Choosing dates

- **One way / Round trip** switches between a single flight and a trip with a return date. Switching back to
  Round trip keeps a return date you had already chosen.
- The weekday is shown next to each date's label ("DEPARTURE Sat").
- The **−** and **+** buttons next to a date move it one day back or forward.
- **Flex departure** (and **Flex return** for round trips) also searches days around your date: up to ±7 days,
  either side or only before or only after. Flexible dates cost almost nothing extra, so use them freely.

## 4. Reading the results

Each result is one compact row:

- **Times** of departure and arrival in **local time** at each airport. "+1" means it arrives the next day.
- **Date**, **total travel time** (time zones included) and **stops**: *Direct* or *1 stop* with the airport.
- **Total price** in euros for one adult (Ryanair's basic fare: no bags, no seat).
- Round trips have an **Out** line and a **Back** line, plus a note such as "10 nights in Barcelona", the leave
  days you'd need and any school break it falls in.

Tap or click a row to open it. You see the full timeline: each flight, its flight number, the wait between
flights, and a separate **Book leg** link for each flight.

The cheapest 20 results are shown first. **Show 20 more** at the bottom adds the next 20. Results further down
are usually pricier or odd routes.

### Warning labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Self-transfer** | Separate tickets. You collect your bags, check in again and go through security. If the first flight is late and you miss the second, Ryanair won't rebook you |
| **Tight layover** / **Tight: 2h 05m** | Less than 2 h 30 min to connect (plus any time to travel between airports). Risky with separate tickets |
| **Long: 9h 40m** | A wait of more than 8 hours |
| **Overnight** | The connecting flight leaves the next day |
| **Airport change** | You land at one airport and fly on from another in the same city (e.g. Luton → Stansted). The label shows the transfer time; at least 2 hours plus the transfer is always allowed |
| **Other airport** | A round trip flies back from another airport in the same city, e.g. out to Stansted and back from Luton. The label shows the travel time between them |
| **Lands in Kaunas** (or another city) | A round trip comes back to another of your home airports within 200 km, e.g. out of Vilnius and back to Kaunas. Only when you picked several home airports |

A summary line above the results shows the cheapest price, the fastest trip and the cheapest direct option.

### Cheaper nearby

When moving the trip by a few days would be noticeably cheaper, one line under the summary says so, for example
"Cheaper nearby: Wed 2 Dec – Thu 10 Dec from €130.87 · €33.58 less". Press it to search those dates.

- It looks up to 3 days either side of your dates, and skips days your search already covered.
- A round trip keeps about the same length: at most one day longer or shorter.
- It appears only for a saving of at least €5 and 10%.
- Near the start or end of a month it looks at fewer days on that side. It isn't shown for Anywhere or for
  searches with very many airports.

If nothing is found, RFF suggests what to try: flexible dates, nearby airports (within 250 km), a one-way
search or Anywhere.

## 5. Booking

- **Book** on a row opens that trip on ryanair.com, with airports and dates already filled in. Check the price
  there before paying, because fares change often.
- **N tickets** instead of Book means the trip is made of separate bookings. Open the row and use each
  **Book leg** link, one per flight.
- Book the legs in order and allow for delays: separate tickets are not protected if you miss a connection.

## 6. Sorting and filtering

Above the results (on phones, a row of chips you can scroll sideways):

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sort**: Cheapest / Fastest / Earliest | Order of the results |
| **Direct only** | Hide trips with a stop |
| **Same airport only** | Hide trips that change airports in a city or come back to a different airport |
| **Layover** | Hide trips with a longer wait than you choose |
| **Max €** | Hide trips above a price |

Sorting and filtering happen instantly in your browser and don't search again.

## 7. Anywhere: cheapest places to fly to

Pick **Anywhere** in the **To** field to list the cheapest destinations from your airport(s) on your dates.
You get one row per destination: the cheapest way there, plus the cheapest direct option if that's different.

- Up to 5 departure airports, and at most 15 airport × day combinations (e.g. 3 airports with ±2 days).
- For round trips, return flights are looked up for the 40 cheapest destinations.
- **List / Map** switches between the list and a map with price pins. The map fits all destinations in view.
  Price labels show for the cheapest ones, and the rest are dots: tap a dot to see the price, or zoom in to see
  more labels. **See flights** in a pin's popup searches that destination.
- Anywhere searches are heavier, so each visitor can run 5 of them per 10 minutes.

## 8. Price calendar and date grid

Open **Price calendar** above the results (not available for Anywhere). Round trips open on the **Date grid**
tab; **Month (outbound)** switches to the month view.

- **Month view** shows the cheapest one-way price for each day of the month, marking direct flights and
  connections, Lithuanian public holidays and school breaks. Tap a day to search it (a round trip keeps its
  length). The arrows change the month.
- **Date grid** (round trips) shows departure days ±3 against return days ±3, with the cheapest round trip in
  each cell. Under each price you see the leave days needed; orange means a pupil would miss school. Tap a cell
  to search those dates.

Calendars for many airports at once show direct flights only, and very large ones (over 100 airport pairs)
aren't available. Narrow the airports down if you see that message.

## 9. Holiday windows (Lithuania)

**Holiday windows** (closed until you open it) lists upcoming Lithuanian long weekends and school breaks, for
example "Christmas to New Year · 11 days off · 4 leave days". Each window shows:

- how many days off it gives and which leave days you'd need (**Free** when none),
- **Best value** when you get at least 2.75 days off per leave day (e.g. 11 days for 4 leave days),
- days a pupil would miss school.

Picking a window searches those dates, or fills them into the form if you haven't chosen airports yet.

## 10. Saved routes, recent searches and sharing

- **Save route** next to the results title keeps the route (airports, not dates). Saved routes appear above
  the recent searches; tapping one searches it on the dates you're looking at, or tomorrow. Up to 10.
- **Recent** shows your last 3 searches. **Clear** forgets them.
- **Sharing**: the page address holds the whole search, including sorting and filters. Copy it to share or
  bookmark, and Back/Forward in the browser work as expected. Shared links show a preview with the route and date.
- **Old links**: a link or bookmark whose departure date has passed opens the same trip on the next same
  weekday, with the same length, and a notice says so ("That link was for Sat 10 Oct, which has passed. Showing
  Sat 14 Nov instead.").

Saved routes, recent searches and your theme choice are kept only in your browser (on that device).

## 11. On your phone

- After a search, the form folds into one line ("Vilnius → London · Fri 27 Nov") so the results are on the
  first screen. **Edit** opens the full form again.
- **Add to home screen**: in Safari, tap Share → *Add to Home Screen*; in Chrome, ⋮ → *Add to Home screen* or
  *Install app*. RFF then opens full screen like an app.
- **Dark mode** follows your phone's or computer's setting. The sun/moon switch at the bottom of the page
  overrides it; switching back to the device's own theme makes RFF follow the device again.

## 12. Limits and things to know

- **One fare per route per day.** Ryanair's public prices give the cheapest flight of each day on each route.
  A connection that would need a pricier flight on one leg can be missed, and on routes with several flights a
  day you see only the cheapest one.
- **Connections** wait between 2 and 12 hours at the stopover airport (more when changing airports).
- **Prices change.** RFF keeps prices for up to 15 minutes. The price on ryanair.com is the one that counts.
- **Search size.** Up to ±7 flexible days, 40 airports on each side and 300 airport × date combinations. Up to
  200 results are kept for sorting and filtering.
- **Speed limits per visitor**: 30 searches a minute, 5 Anywhere searches per 10 minutes, 40 calendar views a
  minute. These keep RFF from overloading Ryanair, which would block everyone.

## 13. Questions and problems

**"Ryanair isn't answering right now…"** Ryanair refused requests for a moment, so RFF waits a minute before
asking again. Try again in a few minutes.

**"You're searching very quickly…" or a message about Anywhere searches.** You reached a per-visitor speed limit.
Wait the time shown.

**"The search was interrupted."** Nothing arrived from the server for 60 seconds, usually a network hiccup.
Press **Try again**.

**The price on ryanair.com is different.** Fares change throughout the day, and RFF shows Ryanair's basic fare
for one adult. Bags, seats and other extras are added on ryanair.com.

**A Book link doesn't open the right flight.** Ryanair sometimes changes its website. Use the route and date from
RFF on ryanair.com directly, and let the site owner know.

**A city or region is missing.** Groups are set up by hand. Ask the site owner to add one (see
[MAINTENANCE.md](MAINTENANCE.md)).
